About

Hi! I'm Breanna, a thumbnail designer and content creator who is passionate about graphic design! I've been creating thumbnails for YouTube videos for over 8 years. I enjoy learning new techniques and ways to make my thumbnails stand out. My goal is to create visually appealing thumbnails that capture the attention of viewers and drive engagement!

Minecraft is a game I've been playing for a long time and that I still enjoy to this day, which is why nearly all of the thumbnails I've created so far are from Minecraft haha. Outside of gaming and content creation, I enjoy going for walks, traveling, spending time with family, and playing with my dog!
